## 📚 Overview

Duckvaulta EdTech AI transforms lengthy study materials, thick PDF textbooks, presentation decks, and educational YouTube videos into concise conceptual summaries, smart flashcards with spaced repetition, and predictive multiple-choice quizzes with instant grading and explanations.

### Core Value Proposition:
**"Ubah 6 Jam Belajar Jadi 1 Jam dengan AI"** — Designed to empower high school students, university undergraduates, and civil service/exam candidates (UTBK/SNBT, CPNS, Kedinasan) to master complex subjects in a fraction of the time.

---

## 🎯 Key Features

1. **📄 Automatic Document Summarizer:**
   * Condenses 80+ page documents into key conceptual takeaways without losing critical facts.
   * Multi-subject interactive demo presets: Biology (Cellular Respiration), Law (Principle of Legality), Computer Science (RESTful Architecture).

2. **🗂️ Smart Flashcards (Spaced Repetition):**
   * Digital memory cards with automated repetition schedules for difficult terms to ensure long-term retention.
   * Interactive flip animations and card deck navigation.

3. **🎯 Multiple-Choice Quizzes & Tryout Simulator:**
   * Automated question generator based on user study notes.
   * Instant feedback, score computation, and comprehensive answer explanations.

4. **🎥 YouTube Video to Study Notes:**
   * Extracts key lecture points, timestamps, and study flashcards directly from YouTube educational video URLs.

5. **🤖 24/7 Personal AI Tutor:**
   * Conversational study buddy that provides step-by-step analogies and explanations for difficult mathematics, science, and legal concepts.

6. **🔐 Multi-Role Authentication & Workspaces:**
   * **Student Portal:** Daily study streak tracker, XP leveling, saved subject binders, and new material uploader.
   * **Admin Mission Control:** Real-time active learner metrics, processed document volume, and automated scheduler telemetry.
